Amarena Fabbri

  • Go to all products
  • Download the catalogue

The original since 1915, unique and inimitable.

A customer favourite, always!

Amarena Fabbri is made from a careful selection of the best fruit, pitted and semi-candied in syrup following the founder's original recipe, which has been handed down from generation to generation since 1915.

Today, as in the past, it remains the favourite of customers for its distinctive, inimitable flavour, as well as for its centuries-old history which makes it an icon of Italian taste. It has always been a symbol of Fabbri1905's quality and tradition, and thanks to its versatility, it boasts countless uses in ice cream as a flavour, as a variegation, a ripple or decoration.

Heart and soul of infinite pastry specialities.

It is available in 4 ­different sizes­, with syrup, drained, whole and in pieces. Its unique flavour is also available in Variegated, Topping, Simplé and Nappage ­versions.
