603 Bib is the floor standing machine for soft gelato, soft ice cream and frozen yogurt.

Thanks to the electronic system for consistency control, it always grants a perfect structure.

The patented peristaltic pump grants high productivity and easy maintenance; it allows to treat any kind of mix, even the dense ones and with small pieces like seeds and grains.

With the Bag-in-Box option, it is possible to connect the pump directly to the bag filled with liquid mix.
